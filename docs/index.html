THE METHOD

How it works in 3 steps

[R,G,B]STEP 01

List every color

The algorithm looks at every single pixel in the photo and notes its color.

Technically: turn pixels into vectors

STEP 02

Find the most popular colors

It groups together colors that look alike and picks one best color to represent each group — until only 16 remain.

Technically: move 16 centroids

STEP 03

Re-color the picture

Every pixel gets swapped for the closest of those 16 colors.

Technically: rebuild with the palette

INSIDE COLOR SPACE

A map of all the colors.

Think of this as a map where similar colors sit close together. Every dot is one pixel from the photo. The bright triangles are the 16 colors the algorithm chose to represent their neighborhoods.

pixel = [ R, G, B ]

Nearby dots look alike. K-Means finds the center of each neighborhood, then uses those centers as the compressed palette.

THE BIG IDEA

Compression is not about keeping everything.
It is about finding what represents everything.
